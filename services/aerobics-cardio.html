Aerobics Classes in Nairobi: Cardio & HIIT Fitness

High-energy aerobics, HIIT and cardio-boxing sessions to boost stamina, burn calories and lift your mood.

Book Aerobics & CardioCall 0703 981 450

Aerobics is one of the most popular ways to get fit in Nairobi. Upbeat, energetic cardio sessions strengthen your heart and lungs, burn a lot of energy and leave you feeling great. They are also more fun than a treadmill.

D Dynamic Fitness aerobics and cardio sessions blend classic aerobic movement with HIIT intervals, bodyweight circuits and cardio boxing inspired by Coach Don Michael Ondiro's martial arts background. Every session is scaled to your fitness, so beginners and regulars can train side by side.

Watch: rooftop shadow boxing

Non-stop shadow boxing is one of the cardio drills Don uses most. No equipment, any space, and your heart rate climbs within a minute. Cardio sessions mix this with aerobic circuits and intervals.

Benefits of aerobics and cardio training

  • Stronger heart and lungs and better everyday stamina.
  • Fat loss support through high energy burn.
  • Better mood and lower stress, thanks to the endorphin rush.
  • Improved coordination from rhythmic and boxing movements.
  • Better sleep and more energy through the day.

A typical 45-minute aerobics session

  1. Warm-up (7 min): marching, arm circles and light rhythmic moves.
  2. Aerobic block (15 min): continuous combinations building in intensity.
  3. Cardio boxing (10 min): jabs, crosses, hooks and kicks in rounds.
  4. HIIT finisher (8 min): short intervals scaled to your level.
  5. Cool-down (5 min): slow walking and stretching.

Aerobics for every level

Every movement has an easier and a harder option. Beginners start with low-impact versions and longer rests; regulars add jumps, speed and shorter rests. If you are new to exercise or managing a health condition, tell your coach so the session can be adjusted. For best results, combine cardio with gym and weight training to keep muscle while you burn fat.

Group aerobics and cardio

Cardio sessions work brilliantly in a group: the energy is contagious. Ask about group and partner sessions for friends, church groups or office teams. Aimed at fat loss? See our weight loss programme. We run cardio sessions for clients across Nairobi, including Kilimani and Westlands.

Tips for your first aerobics class

  • Wear supportive trainers with good cushioning.
  • Bring plenty of water. You will sweat.
  • Eat lightly one to two hours before.
  • Go at your own pace and take the low-impact option when you need it.
  • Come back consistently. Stamina builds fast with regular sessions.

Aerobics & Cardio FAQs

Are aerobics classes good for weight loss?

Yes. Aerobics and HIIT burn a lot of energy and, combined with sensible eating and some strength training, are very effective for fat loss.

I'm not coordinated. Can I still do aerobics?

Absolutely. Moves are taught step by step and kept simple. Coordination improves quickly with practice.

Is there a low-impact option?

Yes. Every session includes joint-friendly alternatives without jumping.

How often should I do cardio?

Two to four cardio sessions a week alongside strength training works well for most people.
