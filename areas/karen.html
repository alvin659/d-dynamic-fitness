Strength training for endurance athletes

Many runners and cyclists only run or ride. Adding two short strength sessions a week is one of the most effective ways to run faster, climb better and avoid common injuries like runner's knee and Achilles problems.

ExerciseBenefit for runners and cyclists
Split squats and step-upsSingle-leg strength and knee stability
Romanian deadliftsHamstring and glute strength
Calf raisesAchilles and calf resilience
Planks and carriesCore stability for better posture late in a race
Hops and boundsElastic power and running economy

Outdoor training in Karen

Karen's open spaces suit outdoor sessions: sprints, shadow boxing, pad work and bodyweight circuits in the fresh air. Training outdoors is also great for mental health. When the weather turns during the long rains, sessions move indoors so your progress does not stop.

Tips for staying fit in Karen

  • Use weekend trail walks near Ngong Road Forest as active recovery.
  • Add two strength sessions a week if you mainly run or cycle.
  • Hydrate well. Long outdoor sessions in the sun dehydrate you quickly.
  • Warm up properly before runs on cool mornings.

Personal Training in Karen: FAQs

Do you coach runners and cyclists in Karen?
