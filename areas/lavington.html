Strength and mobility for healthy ageing

After 30, we gradually lose muscle unless we train to keep it. Strength training and mobility work are some of the most effective ways to stay independent, avoid falls and keep doing the things you love. For older Lavington clients, programmes focus on:

  1. Safe strength training for legs, back and grip.
  2. Balance and single-leg stability.
  3. Joint mobility for hips, shoulders and spine.
  4. Low-impact conditioning that is easy on the joints.

See our mobility and flexibility training and one-on-one personal training pages for more detail.

Private, one-on-one coaching

Some people simply do not enjoy busy gyms. One-on-one coaching means the whole session is about you: your technique, your pace, your goals. It also means sensitive topics like injuries, health conditions and confidence can be discussed openly and planned for properly.

Tips for staying fit in Lavington

  • Walk the quieter residential roads in the early morning for easy daily movement.
  • Schedule training like a business meeting, and protect the slot.
  • Track a few simple markers such as strength, waist measurement and sleep to see progress.
  • Combine strength training with daily mobility for the best long-term results.

Personal Training in Lavington: FAQs

Do you offer home personal training in Lavington?

Ask on WhatsApp with your location and we will confirm whether home sessions are available for your area.

Can older adults train with you?

Yes. Programmes are scaled to each person, with a strong focus on mobility, balance and safe strength.

Is strength training safe for people over 50?

Yes, when properly coached and progressed. Strength training is one of the best things you can do for healthy ageing. Get medical clearance first if you have any health conditions.

Can you work alongside my physiotherapist?

Yes. We are happy to follow your physiotherapist's guidance and build strength once you are cleared to train.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Every programme starts with an assessment and is built from your current fitness level. Beginners are very welcome.

How do I book a session?

Tap the WhatsApp or Call button on any page, or use the contact form. Tell us your goal, location and preferred times, and we will reply to arrange your first session.
