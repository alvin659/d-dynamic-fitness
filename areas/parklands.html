Who we train in Parklands

Parklands is a lively, diverse neighbourhood stretching from Limuru Road across the numbered avenues towards Westlands. Clients include university students, young professionals, families and healthcare workers from the nearby hospitals. Many want effective, affordable training they can keep up for the long term.

  • Students who want to get fit and learn self-defence.
  • Healthcare workers on shifts who need flexible scheduling.
  • Families looking for active, confidence-building activities.

Affordable ways to train

Personal training does not have to mean a big monthly bill. Popular options for Parklands clients include:

  1. Small-group sessions: train with two or three friends and share the cost.
  2. Fewer sessions plus homework: one or two coached sessions a week, with a clear plan for the other days.
  3. Calisthenics-based programmes: no gym membership needed.

Ask on WhatsApp for current rates and group options.

Training for shift workers

Nurses, doctors and other shift workers have some of the hardest schedules to train around. Programmes for shift workers focus on flexibility: shorter sessions, simple routines that can be done before or after a shift, and a strong emphasis on recovery and sleep. If a fixed weekly slot does not work, sessions can be booked week by week where availability allows.

Calisthenics and boxing for students

Bodyweight training and boxing fitness are perfect for students: minimal equipment, maximum results and skills that build confidence. Many students start by learning their first full push-up or pull-up and end up chasing advanced skills like the one-arm push-up. See our calisthenics training and boxing fitness pages.

Tips for staying fit in Parklands

  • Walk where it is safe and practical instead of taking short boda rides.
  • Find a training partner to keep each other accountable.
  • Try the 100 push-ups a day challenge, spread across the day.
  • Plan healthy, affordable meals around beans, eggs, vegetables and whole grains.

Do you offer small-group sessions in Parklands?
